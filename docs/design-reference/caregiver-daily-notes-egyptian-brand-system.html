THE STORY / الحكاية

من نقشة المشربية… لعلامة بتطمن

The layered frame nods to Egyptian mashrabiya screens and the welcoming geometry found in local homes. At the centre, a living notebook/check mark signals organised daily care; the small coral heart adds humanity without depicting people.

Inspired by Egyptian craft, not tourist symbols. No pyramids, flags, people, or generic medical crosses. A distinctive emblem for families, nurses, and hospital teams alike.
