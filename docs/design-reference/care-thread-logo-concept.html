A mark built from the real job of the product: turning everyday observations into a clear, continuous record of care.

THE IDEA — “THE CARE THREAD”
One continuous Nile-like line moves through three record strokes—morning, afternoon, evening—then resolves into a confident check. The line means continuity; the strokes mean daily notes; the check means the care team has noticed, recorded, and followed through.

The line resolves into a check: notes become action. It works for family caregivers, nurses, and hospital teams alike.
