02 / CREATIVE ROUTESFOUR DIFFERENT IDEAS, ONE HUMAN PURPOSE

THE LOGO EXPLORATION

Four ways to express “رفيق”.

Each route starts with a different reading of companionship, continuity and understanding. None relies on a medical symbol.

CONCEPT 01Organic · Human

The Beside

Two forms. One shared space.

Care is not a task performed at someone; it is a space made together.

What it says

Two rounded, complementary forms lean toward a quiet shared centre. The open middle is the room created by trust: present, protective, never enclosing.

Why it fits

It makes companionship the first thing the brand says, before a single feature is mentioned. The paired shapes feel personal to a family and composed enough for a care team.

Strength

The warmest and most immediately human direction. The silhouette stays legible at small sizes and has a natural pattern language.

Watch-out

The paired forms can read as leaves or petals without the wordmark. Consistent proportions and a restrained palette keep it out of generic wellness territory.

CONCEPT 02Continuous · Distinctive

The Daily Thread

A line that carries meaning forward.

Each note is a moment; the thread between moments is understanding.

What it says

One continuous path passes through three small points of attention. It begins with an observation, gathers context, and carries that understanding forward—without drawing a chart or an AI sparkle.

Why it fits

The mark is a compact metaphor for the actual workflow: daily notes become an evidence-backed summary, which can inform a thoughtful next step.

Strength

Strongest link to the product, ownable motion, easy to animate, and memorable as an app icon. It works in a single ink and at 24px when the nodes remain open.

Watch-out

A path can suggest a trend line if overused. Keep it curved and human, never angular or data-dashboard-like.

EN / AR / BILINGUAL / SYMBOL / APP ICONOpen vector concept board ↗
CONCEPT 03Geometric · Intelligent

The Gather

Small pieces become a whole.

A fuller picture emerges when separate observations are held together.

What it says

Four rounded arc-fragments sit around a shared centre. No one fragment claims to be the whole; the negative space is as important as the pieces.

Why it fits

It expresses synthesis and continuity, while staying abstract enough to avoid a medical or monitoring symbol. It is especially effective as a modular brand pattern.

Strength

Excellent system potential for dashboards, note groups, summary cards and motion. It has a precise, modern silhouette.

Watch-out

More conceptual and less emotionally immediate than the companion or thread directions. Avoid adding extra fragments or decorative rings.

EN / AR / BILINGUAL / SYMBOL / APP ICONOpen vector concept board ↗
CONCEPT 04Typographic · Timeless

The Rafeek R

A familiar initial, made personal.

A dependable name becomes a quiet, recognizable signature.

What it says

A custom rounded R is drawn as a single confident gesture; its leg returns outward rather than closing into a rigid block, hinting at a companion who stays alongside.

Why it fits

It puts the name itself at the centre of recognition and gives the English identity a strong shorthand for stationery, product chrome and the app tile.

Strength

Simple, scalable and easy to reproduce in one colour. The wordmark can carry more of the personality than the symbol.

Watch-out

The symbol is Latin-initial-led, so the Arabic wordmark must always be treated as a full, equal lockup—not as a translation tucked underneath.

09 / USAGE & CRAFTKEEP THE MARK QUIET AND CLEAR

REPRODUCTION

Consistency is a form of care.

The mark is a compact path with three moments. Its simplicity depends on breathing room, a stable stroke and clear contrast.

CLEAR SPACE
xx

Keep at least one node diameter of clear space around the symbol and one wordmark cap-height around a full lockup.

MINIMUM SIZE
24 px / symbol
120 px / digital lockup
32 px / icon

Use the symbol alone below 120px lockup width. Do not shrink Arabic wordmarks until counters or dots begin to close.

DO NOT
Stretch
Recolour arbitrarily
Add a heavy shadow
Outline the wordmark
BACKGROUND COMPATIBILITY
Paper
Dark Pine
White / print
Wordmark

Never redraw or re-space the Arabic letters. Use the supplied Arabic SVG lockup.

Colour

Use Pine for the positive mark; white for dark grounds; black for single-colour reproduction.

Scale

Keep the 8-unit core stroke and rounded terminals. Do not add extra nodes to the mark.

Direction

Use the English lockup in LTR contexts, Arabic in RTL contexts, bilingual where both audiences meet.
